# Changelog

## v1.13 (2026-11-07)

### Added

- **FADE.** A checkbox (`F`) and an ASCII slider under OPACITY. With FADE on, a stroke starts at the chosen opacity and dies out along its path, like a fake pen pressure: the further you drag, the less ink it leaves, until it is gone. The slider is the speed (left = long trail, right = short trail). It works with paint, erase, blur and lighten/darken, and every new stroke starts at full strength again. Constants: `FADE_LEN_MAX` / `FADE_LEN_MIN` (stroke length in cells).

## v1.12 (2026-11-07)

## v1.08 (2026-11-07)

### Added

The word timing comes from the synthesizer itself. `VOICE_CAPTIONS = False` hides them; they follow the voice (off when the voice or the sound is off). `VOICE_CAPTION_HOLD` and `VOICE_CAPTION_RGB` tune them.

## v1.07 (2026-11-07)

### Added
- **Voice.** A very raw formant speech synthesizer (in the spirit of the old SAM voices), generated by code with NumPy, no audio files. It says short phrases: the name of the brush or mode you switch to (`paint`, `blur`, `line`, `highlight`, `smart brush`, `smart edge`, `smudge`, `ink bomb`), a funny line when you enter TRU mode (`true mode`, `less slop mode`, `organic mode`, `hand made mode`, `artisanal mode`, `no cheating mode`), a line when a bomb is lit (`splat incoming`, `messy business`, `big splash`, `here comes the mess`, `letters everywhere`, `scatter the letters`), `timelapse start` / `stop` / `cleared`, `gif ready`, `palette saved` / `palette restored`.
  - **VOICE checkbox** next to GRID in the sidebar, or the `V` key (it says `voice off` before going quiet). `Shift+S` (sound off) silences it too. In TRU mode `V` is just typed.
  - tunables at the top of the file: `VOICE_ON_DEFAULT`, `VOICE_VOL`, `VOICE_PITCH`, `VOICE_SPEED`, `VOICE_CRUSH` (gritty low bit depth); `VOICE_LEXICON` (phoneme spelling of the words) and crude spelling rules for any other word.
- Help (`Shift+H`) lists `V`.

### Changed
- **Bomb gradation.** On a dense area (a block of the densest character) the trails of many particles piled up and brought almost everything back to the densest character, with a gradation only near the centre. Now the ink has a ceiling that rises smoothly from the centre to the tip of every ray (`BOMB_FADE_POW`, 1.0) and the trails are weaker (`BOMB_TRAIL_GAIN`, 0.45). Opacity relaxes the ceiling.

### Tests
- 387 checks in total: synthesizer (phonemes, formants, noise, determinism, speed), voice events (modes, TRU lines, timelapse, bomb, palette), toggles (key, checkbox, sound off), bomb gradation on a solid block.

## v1.06 (2026-11-07)

### Added
- **TRU mode** (`TAB`, or the `TRU: TYPE IT [TAB]` button under BOMB): for people who want to draw letter by letter. No brushes at all: the mouse only moves a blinking cursor (hover or click), and everything is typed.
  - type any character the font can draw and it lands at the cursor; the cursor steps right (`TRU_ADVANCE`). `Shift` + number types the shifted symbol as usual.
  - hold `Alt` and press `1` to `0` to set the shading level of the cell under the cursor, in place, from `1` (darkest, empty) to `0` (lightest). The ten keys are spread over however many levels the palette has. Each key press plays the keyboard click.
  - arrows move the cursor, `Home` / `End`, `Enter` (next line), `Backspace` (step back and clear), `Delete`, `Space` (clear). `Ctrl` shortcuts (undo, save...) still work, `F1` shows the help, `Esc` or `Tab` leave the mode.
  - letters that are shortcuts elsewhere (`K`, `M`, `T`...) are just typed in TRU.
  - characters outside the palette are kept as typed (same mechanism as Smart Edge characters), saved to TXT and kept when loading.
- **Palette editor: typing.** With the editor open, type a character to put it into the selected slot; the table highlights it. A character the font cannot draw, or a space, is refused with a message.

### Changed
- Loading a TXT now keeps any character the font can draw as typed, instead of turning everything outside the palette and edge set into empty cells.
- In the palette editor the `+` and `-` keys type those characters (use the `-` / `+` buttons to remove / add levels).
- Help (`Shift+H`) lists the TRU keys.

### Tests
- 352 checks in total: TRU button, layout, keys, mouse only moves the cursor, typing, Shift symbols, Alt levels, arrows, Enter, Backspace, shortcuts do not leak, Ctrl+Z, TXT round trip, cursor drawing, tooltip; typing in the palette editor.

## v1.05 (2026-11-07)

  - edits are live on the canvas; the title shows a red `*` while the palette differs from the saved one.
- Tooltips and a help entry for the new double click on the tone sphere.

### Changed
- The old single-character table popup is replaced by the palette editor (same table, much more control).

## v1.03 (2026-11-07)

### Changed
- **Bomb: the blast ring shrinks during the fuse.** The ring that shows the blast radius is now drawn for the whole fuse, starts at the full radius and closes in faster and faster (like a falling bomb), reaching the centre cell exactly when the explosion sound starts. It gets brighter as it closes (`BOMB_RING_POW` controls the curve).
- **Bomb respects the opacity.** Opacity is now the influence of the wave: at 100% the centre is emptied completely; at 50% only half of the ink is pushed (the centre stays half full) and the debris and trails carry that half; at the minimum the canvas barely changes. Before, only the debris was weaker and the rest of the ink just disappeared.

### Tests
- 252 checks in total: ring radius (start, never grows, accelerates, reaches the centre), ring drawn at the blast radius, opacity scaling.

## v1.02 (2026-11-07)

### Changed
- **Bomb is now a shock wave, like a stone dropped in water.** The wave leaves the centre and travels to the tips; every cell is emptied and its ink starts flying only when the front reaches it, and a faint bright ring rides on the front.
- **Radial smudge model.** `BOMB_RAYS` (18) rays are spread around the circle, each with its own reach (`BOMB_REACH_MIN` to `BOMB_REACH_MAX` of the blast radius, a few long spikes). A cell takes the reach of the rays around it, weighted by angle (`BOMB_RAY_SPREAD`), so neighbouring directions blend smoothly. Few rays still look smooth; more rays give finer streaks.
- **Gradual void.** The centre (`BOMB_CORE`, 25% of the radius) is emptied completely; further out each cell keeps more of its ink the closer it is to the tip of its ray (`BOMB_KEEP_POW`). The ink that was removed is pushed outward along the ray, leaves a trail that gets stronger toward the landing point, and piles up near the tip.
- Blast takes 1.0 s (`BOMB_BLAST_MS`), eased out more gently than before.

### Tests
- 244 checks in total: the void at the centre and its fade toward the tips, wave front timing, ink roughly conserved.

## v1.01 (2026-11-07)

### Added
- **BOMB brush** (`K` or the BOMB button, `Esc` to leave). Click once: the character under the cursor runs through every shade of the palette twice while it blinks red and white faster and faster, and the blast area starts to flicker. Then it explodes (see v1.02 for how the blast works).
- **Blast radius = brush radius** (radius 1 is about 4 cells, each extra step adds 1.5, capped at 60). The bomb always sits on the clicked cell. Opacity scales the strength of the debris.
- **Sounds** generated in code (hiss and crackle with a falling pitch for the fuse, a low thump with a tiny click for the explosion), short and quiet. To use your own, put `sounds/bomb_fuse.wav` and `sounds/bomb_explosion.wav` in the assets folder; the fuse length then follows your file (0.6 to 4 s).
- One `Ctrl+Z` takes back the whole explosion. Several bombs can be lit at once; they explode one after another. While a blast runs, painting, undo and canvas resize wait. The finished explosion adds a timelapse frame.
- Tuning constants at the top of the source: `BOMB_FUSE_MS`, `BOMB_FUSE_LOOPS`, `BOMB_BLAST_MS`, `BOMB_R_BASE`, `BOMB_R_PER_BRUSH`, `BOMB_CORE` (crater size), `BOMB_TRAIL_MIN`, `BOMB_TRAIL_POW`, `BOMB_JITTER`, `BOMB_SOUND_VOL`.

### Tests
- 33 new checks (241 in total): crater, debris range and directions, seed determinism, trail gradient, sounds, key and button, fuse cycle, timeline, undo, two bombs.

## v0.94

### Changed
- **Round brush.** Character cells are taller than wide, so a brush of radius r looked like a tall oval. Brush, blur, thick lines and the brush ring now measure distance in screen pixels and are round on screen (`BRUSH_ROUND_IN_PIXELS = True`; set it to False for the old cell-space circle). The ring is a gap-free outline of exactly the cells the brush paints.
- **Click and drag on the tone sphere and on the palette bar** changes the tone while the mouse is held. Dragging outside the sphere clamps to the nearest cell. The click sound plays only on the first press.
- **Smaller sidebar font** (`UI_FONT_SIZE = 13`) for buttons, info lines and GRID. The drawing font and the mode indicators beside the sphere did not change.

## v0.93

Tone sphere (gradient picker) fixes.

### Fixed
- **Tangled glyphs in the sphere.** The sphere drew characters at half-cell steps, so neighbours overlapped. It now uses a real character grid with a smaller font (`PICKER_FONT_SIZE = 11`), so every glyph has its own cell.
- **Sphere not centered / cut at the top.** The grid is now centered in the frame and the sphere always fits inside it.
- **Weak dark fall-off.** Shading is now "half lambert" normalised over the visible hemisphere, so the sphere uses the full tonal range, from the lightest highlight to a soft dark edge.
- **Click did not pick the clicked character.** It recomputed the tone from the pixel and then highlighted another cell with a similar tone. A click now picks the exact tone of the clicked cell and the highlight stays on that cell.

### Tests
- `tests/smoke_test.py` has 8 new checks for the sphere (43 in total).

## v0.92

Bug fix and cleanup release. The look and feel of the interface did not change.

### Fixed
- **Copy Fit (`Ctrl+Shift+C`) crashed the app.** It used a variable that did not exist (`is_space`), and the error fallback called the same code again.
- **Saves from the .exe went to a temporary folder.** The app changed its working directory to the PyInstaller bundle, so `Ctrl+S` wrote there. Saves now go to a `saves` folder next to the script or the `.exe`.
- **IMPORT button left the mouse "held".** The dialog swallowed the mouse-up, so the app kept painting by itself afterwards. Every dialog now resets the mouse state.
- **`Ctrl+Alt+S` (copy for Slack) never ran.** `Ctrl+S` and plain `S` caught it first.
- **Timelapse frame limit.** The source declared 20000 and silently used 2000. There is now a single `TIMELAPSE_MAX_FRAMES = 2000`.
- **Timelapse recorded noise.** Plain UI clicks injected pause frames, and key presses added frames even when the canvas did not change. Now only real strokes count, and duplicate frames are skipped.
- **Timelapse export lost your frames on failure.** Frames are now cleared only after a successful export. Cancelling the save dialog really cancels.
- **Line mode** now records its final frame in the timelapse.
- **LOAD .txt:** short lines, missing rows and characters that are not in the palette became the lightest tone. They are now empty (the darkest tone, a space).
- **NEW canvas** erased the whole undo history. NEW, LOAD and IMPORT are now undoable.
- Pillow: removed the deprecated `mode=` argument of `Image.fromarray`.

### Changed
- Much faster drawing: glyphs are rendered once and cached (the canvas needs about 4000 per frame). The picture is pixel-identical to v0.91.
- Timelapse GIF export is faster and uses about 3x less memory (8-bit grey frames, repeated frames rendered once).
- `PAL.txt`, `startup.txt` and the sounds moved to `assets/`. Copies placed next to the script or the `.exe` override them.
- The window shrinks when the screen is too small for it (`AUTO_FIT_WINDOW`).
- The version is shown in the sidebar (bottom left) and the file name carries it (`ASCIIPAINT_v092.py`).
- A crash now writes `crash.log` next to the app.
- New `run_asciipaint.bat` / `.sh`, `build_release.bat`, `build_onefile.bat`. They find the newest `ASCIIPAINT_v*.py` and read its version.

### Added
- `tests/smoke_test.py`, a headless test of the main features.
- `README.md` rewritten, `LICENSE` (MIT), `.gitignore`, this changelog.
